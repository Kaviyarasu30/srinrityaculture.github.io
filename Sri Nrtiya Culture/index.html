About the academy

Classical technique, taught with patience — for every age that wants to begin.

Since 2019, Sri Nritya Culture has trained dancers of all ages in Bharathanatyam — the youngest taking their first adavu, and adults returning to a childhood dream. Classes run both from our Madurai studios and live online, so distance is never the reason a student doesn't begin.

  • Traditional Guru–Shishya method, adapted for today's classroom and screen
  • Structured pathway from beginner adavus to Nattuvangam and Abhinaya
  • Regular assessments and real stage opportunities, not just exams
  • Training aligned to affiliated grade syllabus and internationally recognised certification
